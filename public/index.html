    Dive in and clean the sea

    Microplastics are pieces of plastic smaller than 5 mm. Many start as ingredients in things we use every day. You'll take 5 quick lessons. After each lesson, the claw machine scans the ocean and lights up the items that contain microplastics. Grab as many as you can. Your turn ends when you miss.
